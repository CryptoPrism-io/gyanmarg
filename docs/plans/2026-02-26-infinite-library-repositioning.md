# Polymind Repositioning: "The Infinite Library"

**Date**: 2026-02-26
**Status**: Implemented

## Problem

Polymind was positioned as a retention/flashcard/gamification app ("Finally Retain the Ideas That Matter"). The actual product is far more compelling — 76 curated domains of human knowledge, 4,700+ immersive lessons, spanning everything from ancient Vedic wisdom to quantum physics. The old positioning undersold the product and attracted the wrong audience.

## New Positioning

**Core proposition**: Polymind is the world's most immersive reading experience across every domain of human knowledge.

**Tagline**: "Read Everything Under the Sun"

**Key messaging pillars**:
- 76 worlds of knowledge
- The Infinite Library
- Cross-domain discovery
- Curated for the curious mind

## Note on Science/How-To Pages

The `/science` and `/how-to` pages still contain educational content about spaced repetition and the forgetting curve. This is intentional — these are informational pages that explain the learning science methodology. The product still uses spaced repetition under the hood; it's just no longer the marketing identity.
