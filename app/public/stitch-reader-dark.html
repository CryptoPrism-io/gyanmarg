The Power of
Negative Visualization

The Stoic practice of Premeditatio Malorum is not a descent into pessimism, but a sophisticated psychological tool for resilience. By mentally rehearsing potential hardships, we strip them of their power to surprise us.

Consider the things you hold most dear. Imagine, for a fleeting moment, their absence. This contrast doesn't breed fear; it illuminates the profound value of your present reality, transforming dull routine into vivid appreciation.
